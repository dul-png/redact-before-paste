Privacy and limits

  • Everything happens in this tab. The page's Content Security Policy blocks network requests once it has loaded.
  • Nothing is stored. Closing the tab discards your text and the list of placeholders.
  • TFN, ABN, ACN, Medicare and card numbers are only redacted if they pass their check digits, so invoice numbers and amounts stay as they are.
  • Names are detected after a title (Mrs, Dr), a greeting (Dear, Hi), a label (Client:, From:) or a sign-off, and then wherever else they appear. Other names can be missed; add them under Settings.
  • Australian formats only.
  • Browser extensions with access to every site can read this page.
